# Customer memory is Postgres rows, not files

Decided in [Customer memory: fields, expiry and deletion](https://github.com/Daanish2003/bot-sdk/issues/1158), and approved in [spec #1170](https://github.com/Daanish2003/bot-sdk/issues/1170).

A Representative's memory of a Customer lives in Postgres rows in the Representative feature. A Representative has no Computer to keep files on. Customer memory has to be read only for the Customer a Turn serves, corrected by the owner and really deleted, and rows with a known owner and Customer can do that. ADR 0022 stays as it is: it's about a Bot's memory, which is files on its Computer.

- **Three layers.** A typed profile, summaries that expire and link to transcripts, and live reads that are never copied.
- **One writer.** Only a pass writes it, as a job after a Customer thread closes. It sees numbered lines and returns what to write, and code stores it, following ADR 0022's Memory pass. A Representative has no memory tool, so an injected instruction in a Turn can't rewrite a Customer's memory.
- **A summary per closed thread.** A thread closes by going idle or through a Customer merge, and both come to one seam that queues the pass. The row is unique per thread, so a retry can't write a second one and a Bot's result arriving after close can't rewrite it. A thread the owner held when a merge closed it gets none, because undoing the merge reopens it held. Nor does a thread closed by undoing a merge for holding two people's words, which would put one person's words in the other's memory. A summary finds its Customer through its thread, so it follows a merge.
- **Through a Customer merge.** A merge moves no profile value. The survivor reads its own and the absorbed Customer's as a union, `confirmed` beating `claimed`, then the newest, so undoing it gives each Customer its own values with nothing to restore. The owner corrects only the survivor's own: clearing a value there keeps an empty row, which hides what the merge brought and reads as nothing, so what was cleared doesn't show through and stays cleared after an undo. A pass writes each value where undoing would leave it: to the Customer the cited line's sender came from, otherwise the one the thread came from, not the survivor. No pass beats a confirmed value of the owner's that the survivor reads, and a claim can't bring back what the owner cleared on a survivor. A summary stays with its thread, so a thread both people spoke on during a merge keeps its summary on the thread's Customer after an undo, for the owner to read, and a Turn leaves it out, as it does any summary of a thread holding an address the Customer doesn't have now, when that address is another Customer's, the thread holds a second address, or a merge of this Customer's was undone. Otherwise the address is the Customer's own since corrected, and the summary counts. With no record of which merge put which words on a thread, the cost falls on the side of leaving out: after an undone merge, or on a thread with two addresses, a corrected address loses its summaries from Turns, and the owner still reads them.
- **The pass's Model.** It runs on the Release's Model, with no Bot, and its spend is recorded under its own usage kind, `customer_memory`. If the Release names no Model, no Model is called, and the skip is recorded as a row with no text, as it is when the Model can't run or every try fails. It holds no transaction across its Model call.

## Considered Options

- **Files, as a Bot's memory is.** A Representative has no Computer, and a file can't guarantee that a delete reached every copy.
- **A Representative writing memory during a Turn.** Anything a Customer says could then instruct it to write.

## Consequences

- **A limit on deletion.** The pass's Model reply is saved in its job's Checkpoint so that a retry doesn't call the Model again, and Model calls recorded in job Checkpoints last 7 days after success and 30 after failure. Deleting a Customer doesn't reach them. That's accepted because the lab is never deployed, and a real deployment would have to revisit it. The pass's own Checkpoints hold only its reply, not the lines it was shown, though a reply can repeat what they said.
- **A second limit on deletion.** _Amended 2026-11-04 ([#1287](https://github.com/Daanish2003/bot-sdk/issues/1287), decided by the owner on that ticket):_ a Gap's `question` is the Model's wording of something a Customer asked, and `representative_gap` has no link to the Customer, so deleting a Customer doesn't reach it. The owner chose not to add a link, and the delete dialog names it under "What stays". That's accepted for the same reason as the Checkpoints.
- **Messages left by an undone merge go too.** _Amended 2026-11-04 ([#1287](https://github.com/Daanish2003/bot-sdk/issues/1287), decided by the owner on that ticket):_ while a merge stands, the absorbed Customer can speak on the survivor's thread, and an undo leaves those messages there. Deleting that person removes messages to or from their identities on other Customers' threads, along with those threads' summaries, any pass queued for them and hand-offs from them, and removes a thread with nothing left. They are found by address across all the owner's other Customers, because later merges can have moved the thread. The cost is that if the owner edited an address off one Customer and later gave it to the one being deleted, the first Customer's old messages at that address go too. The limit is that an address the deleted Customer no longer holds isn't searched, as no history of past addresses is kept, so their messages at it stay.
- **What redaction reaches, and its limits.** _Amended 2026-11-04 ([#1288](https://github.com/Daanish2003/bot-sdk/issues/1288), spec [#1170](https://github.com/Daanish2003/bot-sdk/issues/1170), decided by the owner on that ticket):_ deleting a Customer redacts their Assignments by the hand-off's Customer id, so delivered and expired threads are covered (the `assignments` service's `redact`, ADR 0029). It follows them to their child Assignments and to what a Turn wholly on them set going: the Assignments it opened, its Check-ins, its Helpers, its Approvals and the Triggers they left. A Turn wholly on them loses its Model side, and its Messages' text is replaced. A Turn that took other work too keeps that work's notice and its Messages, but its whole Model side goes, as it can't be told apart: the owner's decision on the ticket, taking the loss of that other work's lines over keeping any of the Customer's. An open Approval from a Turn wholly on them is denied and redacted, the owner's exception to ADR 0030, so no action runs for a deleted Customer, and a yes whose job hasn't reached its start marker is taken back. It wakes no one, and its card in the Conversation loses its text. A call past that marker may have run, so it isn't called denied: it stays allowed and redacted, a retry of it is cancelled, and one running finishes, keeping no result and waking no one. One from a Turn on other work, a mixed Turn included, stays pending and still runs on a yes: its stored call and text are kept whole, because what runs must be what the owner sees, so it can quote the Customer, and the delete dialog names this under "What stays". Another Turn that read one by id loses only those lines. Everywhere else in those Bots' records since the first of the Assignments came, in Turns, Messages, Summaries and Prompt starts, verbatim copies go. A Turn running through the delete writes its transcript at its end, so its end queues the redaction again. It rewrites old history, so the Bot's next Model call misses the Provider's cache once. The limits the owner accepted on the ticket, for the same reason as the Checkpoints: a verbatim copy means a passage of 12 or more characters that no other Assignment of the owner holds, so a rewording, a shorter passage or a shared one stays, and the delete dialog names rewording under "What stays". A Bot's Message from before the Assignment came and a Message's choice and control parts aren't reached. Nor are job Checkpoints and payloads, files a long Trigger's text spilled to on the Computer, or anything a Channel already delivered. A hand-off whose `customer_id` stayed NULL in the migration's backfill, an older delivered or test-conversation one, can't be found. Further limits of the build, not on the list the owner accepted: a Helper or a Check-in's Turn still running through the delete goes on working. What it then writes stays, and an Approval it then asks for stays pending and can run on a yes, since no second pass follows it. A call the delete catches already running finishes, and it can act for that Customer. A yes to a waiting call in the half second before the delete can still run in its Turn, which holds the call in memory. A Turn on other work too, running through the delete, keeps copies in what it sends afterwards, as the second pass no longer has the words to look for.
- The pass costs one Model call per closed thread, against the owner's Quota when it's on Host credentials.
- A closed thread with a Release that names no Model has no summary, even though the Release's Turns run on the Host's default Model.
