## What it is

A dot is "an always-on agent in ChatGPT" that "can keep making progress between conversations". [G] It runs on GPT-6 Astra and has its own cloud computer. [R][P] OpenAI names no other Model for it. [O]

Dots launched in late September 2026 for Pro users aged 18 and over, outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, and for Business Premium users. Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare get a beta that is off until an admin turns it on. [G][R] Each person gets one dot. More dots, and more speed or monthly work per dot, are promised for later. [G]

## Gist

A dot is close to our Bot's shape: always on, with a cloud computer, auto-review, approvals, Routines and memory. Ours is ahead on how visible memory is, on Demonstration, and on choosing Models.

The gaps are:

- Slack, Teams and SMS Channels
- voice calls
- native apps and a task overview (task threads, In progress / Scheduled / Completed)
- user-written Custom Rules, with pre-approval and hand-off classes
- a read-only proactive-research mode over connected apps
- access to the user's own computer
- payments
- a whole-Bot pause and reset
- Bots working together as a Team
