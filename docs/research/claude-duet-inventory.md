## What it is

Duet is a research preview, by waitlist, for US Pro and Max subscribers. Team and Enterprise plans get it later. [A][P][S]

It is an always-on agent that runs on Claude Opus 5.5. You give it instructions by typing or speaking, and it does the work in your connected apps on its own cloud computer. [A][S]

The model can't be chosen. Duet decides how much effort a job needs. [A][S]

## Other Duet points worth noting

- **Safety:** A dedicated classifier blocks sharing the strictest data tier, such as card and government ID numbers. Health and finance data is shared only on explicit instruction. Prompt-injection defenses come from Anthropic's browser-use research. It has had red teams, an external penetration test and a bug bounty. [A] Our Bot's equivalent is Auto-review, which treats only the user's own Messages as permission, plus Secrets. It has no data-tier classifier.
- **Not possible:** Phone calls and texts. It also can't see all of the user's projects or local files, and it can read Claude work but not change it. [S]
- **Known weaknesses:** It can misjudge urgency, miss items that arrive between checks, and do personal errands less well than work tasks. [A][P][S]

## Gist

Duet is our Bot's shape, packaged as a consumer product: one thread, a cloud computer, connectors, approvals, background checks and a shared usage budget.

Our Bot already has the core (Computer, Routines and Check-ins, Auto-review, Memory, Skills, interrupt and Stop). The gaps are in the product layer:

- voice input
- native mobile apps and push notifications
- a default morning brief and app polling
- topic channels and a Tasks/Make view
- asynchronous, deferred Approvals and actions that are refused outright
- a large connector marketplace and payments
